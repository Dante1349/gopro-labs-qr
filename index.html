Mission 1 Pro — scan one QR

If the camera says “timecode could not be synced” it did not read a Labs command (stock Mission 1 treats every QR as Quik time-sync).
  1. Rear LCD on (not the front screen only).
  2. Swipe to a Video Mode — not Photo / Nightlapse Photo.
  3. Phone: light mode, brightness up. Fill the camera with this one QR.
  4. Success is a checkmark. Then try the test button first.
  5. If the test button also shows timecode: reinstall Labs — gopro.github.io/labs (Mission 1 Pro).
